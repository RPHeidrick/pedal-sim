# Blues OD: design notes

**Designer:** Richard Heidrick
**Files:** LTspice `circuits/ltspice/blues-od.asc` (drawn by me), site netlist `circuits/blues-od.cir`

## What it is

An overdrive with two op amp stages into a diode clipper:

* Op amp: **TL072** (I chose this instead of the TL082 used in some versions of this circuit)
* Clipping: 1N4148 silicon diodes
* Controls: Gain (100k) and Tone (100k)
